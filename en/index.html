Partnerships

The Autonomous Systems Laboratory (LSA) at PUCRS recognizes the strategic importance of partnerships between academia and industry as a driving force for innovation and real impact on society. With over 15 years of experience bridging academic research and practical applications, LSA puts its consolidated expertise in robotics, artificial intelligence, and embedded systems at the service of the productive sector. To this end, we provide our infrastructure and specialized knowledge to foster collaborative projects in areas such as robotics, automation, Internet of Things (IoT), and mobile technologies, directly contributing to strengthening the innovation ecosystem.
